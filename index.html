Research interests

My focus is making crops more resilient to climate stress, from the field and greenhouse to the genome.

Abiotic stress tolerance in crops

Salinity, drought and heat stress mechanisms in rice and other cereals.

Plant molecular biology and functional genomics

Gene expression, QTL mapping and marker-assisted selection.

Plant bioinformatics and computational biology

GWAS, omics data integration and comparative genomics.

Climate-resilient agriculture and food security

Stress-tolerant varieties for tropical and subtropical environments.

Agronomy and crop physiology

Crop growth, yield formation and nutrient use efficiency.

Sustainable agriculture and soil health

Precision agriculture, soil microbiome interactions and eco-friendly crop management.
